**Projects**

**coffeecoach.app**
A web-app idea to help people improve their coffee brewing through structured feedback and data-driven suggestions. I started building it because I was personally trying to get better at brewing and realized most advice online is scattered and inconsistent. That pushed me into collecting high-quality data and implement a decision tree through them to help brewers. *(metrics: 64 daily users)*

**buildinpublic-x**
GitGub commits over the day → twitter/bluesky posts every night. Using this, developers can build in public without the friction of manually posting updates. Requires no server, backend, no SaaS, this tool lives in yout GitHub itself. The project gained traction quickly after sharing online and helped validate that devs want lightweight, automated storytelling tools.

**youtube-rag-scraper**
A pipeline that extracts, processes, and structures high-quality knowledge from YouTube channels for use in RAG systems. I built this after realizing YouTube has some of the best domain-specific content, but it’s hard to query or reuse effectively. The project focuses on turning unstructured video transcripts into something searchable and usable for downstream AI applications. *(metrics: 55 GitHub stars)*

**splitwala**
A simple chatbot that lives in your WhatsApp groups and helps split bills between friends. Built out of personal need while managing shared expenses, aiming to keep the UX minimal and fast. Focus was on solving a real, everyday problem rather than building something overly feature-heavy. *(metrics: hundreds of active daily users)*
