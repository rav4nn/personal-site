## How I measured

- **Accuracy** is the share of words that match what I read. Case, punctuation and "um" don't count. Red words are wrong, struck-through words are missing.
- **Speed** is the time from the end of my speech to the last character in the text box. Every app gets the same 0.3 s between the end of speech and the stop key.
- **RAM** is the peak memory of all the app's processes during the take. Apple Dictation runs inside macOS, so it has no number.
- Colors: accuracy is green from 97%, yellow from 90%. Speed is green under 1 s, yellow under 2 s. RAM and price compare the apps with each other.
- **AI** (cleanup) apps send the transcript through an LLM before they paste. **Raw** (word for word) apps paste what the speech model heard.
- Prices are from each app's site in October 2026. Earlier runs the same day gave numbers within a few percent.
