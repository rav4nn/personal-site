## What I use now: VoiceInk

Aqua Voice won on accuracy, but I ended up on [VoiceInk](https://tryvoiceink.com/?atp=rav4nn). It is so fast that the text feels instant, and that matters more to me than the last few percent. It runs [Parakeet TDT 0.6B v3](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v3), NVIDIA's speech model, on the Mac itself.

I use it in two modes, one key each:

- **Raw** (Right Option): Parakeet only, word for word. I use it for agent prompts, where the model reads my words as they are.
- **Clean** (Fn): Parakeet, then a cleanup pass by Qwen 3.8 27B on Groq. It fixes names, emails and punctuation in about half a second. I use it for messages and emails.

Both modes share a list of word replacements for the names Parakeet gets wrong.

## How I measured

- **Prompts.** Prompt 1 is short and has an email address. Prompt 2 is long and loose, the way I really talk. Prompt 3 is a long release task with product names.
- **Accuracy** is the share of words that match what I read. Case, punctuation and "um" don't count.
- **Speed** is the time from the end of my speech to the last character in the text box. Every app gets the same 0.3 s between the end of speech and the stop key.
- **RAM** is the peak memory of all the app's processes during the take. Apple Dictation runs inside macOS, so it has no number.
- Colors: accuracy is green from 97%, yellow from 90%. Speed is green under 1 s, yellow under 2 s. RAM and price compare the apps with each other.
- **AI** (cleanup) apps send the transcript through an LLM before they paste. **Raw** (word for word) apps paste what the speech model heard.
- Prices are from each app's site in October 2026. Earlier runs the same day gave numbers within a few percent.
